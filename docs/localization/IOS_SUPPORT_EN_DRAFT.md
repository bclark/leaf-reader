# Leaf iOS support baseline

Status: DRAFT for owner approval and translation. The live support destination remains English: https://github.com/bclark/leaf-reader/issues

Import comics from Files using Load Comic, or choose From SMB Drive to connect to your own server. Leaf supports CBZ, CBR, and PDF. Imported copies remain on your device for offline reading. Leaf does not include comic books.

If SMB cannot connect, check Wi-Fi, the server address and share, your account permissions, and Leaf’s Local Network permission in iOS Settings. You can still import local files. Forget Connection removes the saved SMB password from the device Keychain.

Backups include reading progress, metadata, reading lists, and preferences; they do not include comic files. Keep your original comic files separately. Deleting an imported comic removes Leaf’s copy, not your original file.

Leaf follows iOS’s per-app language setting and standard language matching. There is no language selector inside Leaf. Mexican Spanish is currently an unreviewed draft; its availability in a development build is not a claim of release readiness.

Optional tips support development and unlock nothing. Apple handles purchases. If a tip is pending, you may keep reading. Check Apple purchase history before trying again if its outcome is unclear. Choosing another tip is a separate purchase and does not cancel the pending one.

For help, open an issue at the English support destination. Do not post passwords, server credentials, private comics, receipts, or other personal information. Include your Leaf version, iOS version, device model, and steps to reproduce the problem. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information before sharing them.
