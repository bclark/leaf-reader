# Proposed English iOS listing

Status: DRAFT — awaiting owner approval. Not entered in App Store Connect. This is an iOS-specific baseline, not a copy of the Android listing. Do not change the App Store primary language without approval.

Name: Leaf: Comic Reader

Subtitle: Your comics, offline

Promotional text: Bring your CBZ, CBR, and PDF comics into Leaf. Organize series and reading lists, then settle in with page turns and layouts that suit your screen.

Description:

Read your own comics with Leaf, a free comic reader for iPhone and iPad.

Import CBZ, CBR, and PDF files from Files, or connect to your own SMB drive to choose comics to download. Imported comics stay on your device and can be read offline.

Keep your library organized with series and custom reading lists. Pick up where you left off, mark comics as read or unread, and choose left-to-right or right-to-left reading. Use single pages or two-page spreads, with separate portrait and landscape settings. Fit the whole page or fit its width for smaller text.

Choose a light, dark, or system appearance, with true black available in dark mode. Export and restore backups of reading progress, metadata, reading lists, and preferences. Backups do not contain comic files.

All features are free. Optional one-time tips support development and unlock nothing. No Leaf account is required. Leaf has no advertising, analytics, or developer-operated cloud library.

You supply your own comic files. No comic catalog is included.

Keywords: comics,manga,cbz,cbr,pdf,offline,reader,reading,list,series
