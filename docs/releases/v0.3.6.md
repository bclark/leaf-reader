# A fresh look and a playful way back

Leaf 0.3.6 gives you more ways to make your library feel like yours.

- **Choose your appearance.** Use Light or Dark mode, or let Leaf follow your device. AMOLED Black is still available for dark mode.
- **Pick your app icon.** Choose the light or dark Leaf illustration independently of the app theme. Supported Android launchers can also use the themed icon.
- **Flick a comic away.** Swipe downward and release to return to your library. A quick flick carries the comic off in your swipe's direction; a short, slower swipe springs back. Pinch zoom and page scrolling keep their usual gestures.
- **Enjoy cleaner cover stacks.** Series and lists have softer shadows and no black cover outlines. Back covers are gently dimmed, then brighten as you pull the front issue away.
- **Find your lists in All.** Reading lists now appear alongside comics and series, including empty lists. Search for a list by its name.

## Downloads

These Android release files are **unsigned** and need signing before installation or store upload. No signing key is configured for this repository's release builds.

- The **full APK** includes ZIP, RAR, PDF, and SMB support.
- The **F-Droid APK** is the F-Droid flavor, without the full flavor's 7-Zip dependency.
- The **AAB** is an Android App Bundle for preparing a store submission.
- The mapping files are for developers investigating release-build errors.

This release publishes Android artifacts. The matching iOS interaction and appearance work is documented in the [iOS handoff](https://github.com/bclark/leaf-reader/blob/v0.3.6/docs/IOS_ANDROID_036_HANDOFF.md).
