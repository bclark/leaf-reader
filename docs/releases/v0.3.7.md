# Jellyfin library integration and progress sync

Leaf 0.3.7 connects your local comic reader to your Jellyfin media server.

- **Browse and download from Jellyfin.** Connect directly to your Jellyfin server over HTTPS or local LAN HTTP. Browse your comic libraries, collections, and series folders, and download issues for offline reading.
- **Two-way reading progress sync.** Keep your reading spot in sync between Leaf and Jellyfin. Reading a downloaded comic updates your progress on Jellyfin automatically, and importing an issue preserves your current server progress and read status.
- **Accessibility and touch refinements.** All interactive buttons, library rows, and selection checkboxes now adhere to 48dp minimum touch targets and provide explicit TalkBack accessibility roles (`Role.Button`, `Role.Checkbox`).
- **Privacy and store compliance.** Clean local backup rules ensure your database transfers seamlessly between devices without leaking credentials to unencrypted cloud backups. 100% offline-first reading with zero telemetry.

## Downloads

These Android release files are **unsigned** and need signing before installation or store upload. No signing key is configured for this repository's release builds.

- The **full APK** includes ZIP, RAR, PDF, SMB, and Jellyfin support.
- The **F-Droid APK** is the F-Droid flavor, without proprietary or non-free dependencies.
- The **AAB** is an Android App Bundle for preparing a store submission.
- The mapping files are for developers investigating release-build errors.

This release publishes Android artifacts. The matching iOS Jellyfin integration and progress sync specifications are documented in the [iOS handoff](https://github.com/bclark/leaf-reader/blob/v0.3.7/docs/IOS_ANDROID_037_HANDOFF.md).
