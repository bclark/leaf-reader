Leaf links and embeds AMSMB2.framework dynamically. libsmb2 is compiled into that framework.
Leaf source and build instructions: https://github.com/bclark/leaf-reader
To rebuild with a modified library, use a modified checkout of the pinned AMSMB2 source
(including its pinned libsmb2 submodule), replace the package source in iosApp/project.yml,
regenerate the project, run pod install and build/re-sign Leaf.xcworkspace with your team.
Do not statically link the combined library into Leaf. Source distribution and App Store
license/export obligations must be checked before publishing this experimental feature.
