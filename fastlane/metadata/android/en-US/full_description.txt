Leaf is an offline comic and manga reader for Android. Open your CBZ, CBR, or PDF comics and enjoy smooth whole-page reading, customizable single-page and two-page spreads, interactive page transitions, and local network drive imports.

Features:

* Archive Support — Reads CBZ (ZIP) archives and PDF files across all builds, plus CBR (RAR/RAR5) archives in full releases.
* Reading Layouts — Single-page view or two-page spreads in landscape and portrait, with smart cover page handling and fit-to-page / fit-to-width modes.
* Interactive Transitions — Smooth page turns with interactive 3D page curl or side-by-side sliding.
* Jellyfin — Browse your server libraries, download comics, import server progress and report reading progress back. Reporting is best effort; failed offline writes are not durably retried.
* Local SMB Drive — Connect directly to your home NAS or local network share to browse and download comic archives directly into your library.
* Portable Backup & Restore — Export and restore your reading progress, reading lists, and layout preferences as JSON without exposing sensitive credentials or local paths.
* Privacy-First & Offline — No accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no cloud dependencies. Optional network access connects directly to your configured SMB and Jellyfin servers. Jellyfin-linked reading reports progress to your server.
