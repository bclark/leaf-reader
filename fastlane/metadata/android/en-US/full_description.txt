Leaf is a free, open-source comic and manga reader for Android. Bring your own comics, choose your reading layout, and pick up where you left off.

READ OFFLINE
Open CBZ, supported CBR archives, and PDF files from your device. Download comics from your own Jellyfin server or SMB network share to read without a connection.

MAKE THE PAGE FIT
Switch between single pages and two-page spreads in portrait or landscape. Choose fit-to-page or fit-to-width, smart cover handling, and page-curl or sliding transitions.

KEEP YOUR PLACE
Save your reading progress and organize comics into reading lists. Export and restore your reading progress, lists, and layout preferences.

CONNECT YOUR OWN LIBRARY
Browse comics on your Jellyfin server or SMB share. These connections are optional: you can use Leaf with local files without a server or a Leaf account.

Leaf can import Jellyfin reading progress and report progress to your server. Linked reading updates that cannot be confirmed are kept on this device for retry. After reconnecting, choose Sync reading position in comic details. If positions conflict, choose which one to keep. Changes made in other clients may need an explicit sync.

BRING YOUR OWN CONTENT
Leaf does not include comics or provide a Jellyfin server. Remote libraries require a server or network share you have permission to access. CBR compatibility depends on the archive.
